Privacy Policy

Amusheriff · Amu Sharif Arabiyan Majlis furniture storefront

This policy explains what the Amusheriff app does with information when you browse our furniture catalogue, save favourites, or contact the shop. It applies to the Android app and to this website.

Who we are

Amusheriff (Amu Sharif Arabiyan Majlis) is a furniture shop. The app is a catalogue and a contact channel, not an online checkout. For any question about this policy, write to muktarbiya574@gmail.com.

Information the app uses

What Why Where it goes
Catalogue content you request To show categories, products, prices and photos Our database host (Supabase) and its image CDN, over HTTPS
Favourites To remember the pieces you liked Stored on your device only
Language and appearance choice To keep the app in your language and theme Stored on your device only
A contact button you tap (phone, WhatsApp, Telegram, e-mail) To put you in touch with the shop The app you choose (dialer, WhatsApp, Telegram, mail app) handles the message; we receive it there
Owner sign-in (shop staff only) To let the owner edit the catalogue An authenticated session with our database host

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, address, or payment details.
  • It does not use advertising networks, advertising identifiers, or cross-app tracking.
  • It does not read your contacts, calendar, photos, or location. The camera and files are only opened if shop staff choose a product photo to upload.
  • It does not sell or rent information to anyone.

Crash reports

If the app stops unexpectedly, Android may offer to send a crash report to Google Play. That report contains the technical error and device model, never your personal content, and it is only sent if you agree.

Permissions

The app requests internet access only. Opening a phone number, chat, or e-mail uses the Android app you already have installed; the storefront itself never dials, messages, or mails on your behalf.

Keeping and deleting data

Favourites and language settings stay on your device and disappear when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Messages you send by phone, WhatsApp, Telegram or e-mail are kept by those services and by the shop for as long as needed to answer you; ask us at muktarbiya574@gmail.com to delete a conversation and we will.

Children

The app is a furniture catalogue for the general public. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Service providers

We use Supabase (database, file storage, and image delivery) and Google Play (app distribution) to run the app. They process data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
